# Briefings of 2026-10-16

The demo's saved briefings: three free PreReason briefings as a free key was served them on 2026-10-16 at 09:28 UTC, macro.snapshot, cross.correlations and btc.context. Each is kept twice, as markdown (`format=markdown`) and as JSON (`format=json`, with its `_timing` field removed). `catalog.json` is PreReason's keyless catalog (`GET https://api.prereason.com/api/context/briefings`), read the same minute, and `saved-at.txt` is when they were read. `journal/macro.snapshot/` keeps macro.snapshot as it stood on each of the 29 days before, read with `date=` on the same free key at 10:23 UTC, beside the read of the day itself, so the demo's board draws its 30 days.

The demo reads them in place of PreReason, so it needs no key and calls nothing. They are kept exactly as served on that day, so a later day's briefings may say other words.

The figures in these briefings are their publishers', as PreReason served them on 2026-10-16, and are not covered by this repository's licence.
